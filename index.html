05 / Projects

Selected projects

From Computation to Information to Accuracy: On the Feasibility of Adaptive Visual Token Pruning

Investigates question-adaptive visual token pruning through oracle sparsity labeling, text-only and multimodal sparsity classifiers, and evaluation across seven vision-language benchmarks.

https://github.com/tsjyma/qasparse
BACH: Bridging Agent and Chord Harmony for Long-form Accompaniment Generation

Combines a local chord planner with LLM agents for structure, arrangement, instrument writing, and critique. Generates piano, bass, and drum accompaniment from a LilyPond melody, with score and MIDI rendering.

https://github.com/tsjyma/music-agent
